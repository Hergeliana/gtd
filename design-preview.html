What changed

Typography

System UI stack instead of Arial — sharper on every OS. Tighter letter-spacing on headings, comfortable line heights (1.4–1.55). Anti-aliasing on.

Elevation, not borders

Cards use layered soft shadows (shadow-sm, -md, -lg) instead of 2px colored borders. Status shows as a 4px left-accent bar — visible but quiet.

Refined palette

  • 2min — slightly desaturated orange
  • Defer — modern blue
  • Projects — forest green
  • Overdue — muted red

Sidebar polish

Section headers (Workflow / Reference), colored dots, hover backgrounds, and a left accent bar on the active tab. Count pills sit on the right.

Motion

150ms transitions on hover, focus, and active states. Cards lift gently on hover; buttons darken; capture input shows a focus glow.

Spacing on a 4px grid

4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 — no more arbitrary 7px or 13px gaps. Predictable rhythm.

Inputs

The capture box gets a soft border and a 4px focus ring at 10% opacity — accessible and modern.

Pills, not text

Due dates, contexts, projects, and checklist counts shown as pill badges, color-keyed to their meaning.

Empty states

Friendly icon + short message instead of italic "Nothing here yet."

Accessibility

Visible focus rings (2px / 30% accent), proper contrast on muted text, larger touch targets (44px min on mobile).

Performance ideas

  • Inline critical CSS, defer the rest
  • Replace inline Babel transform with a pre-compiled bundle
  • Use content-visibility:auto on long lists
